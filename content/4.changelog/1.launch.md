🚀 Excited to announce the public launch of our Free speech platform! This milestone is the result of decades of design, development, and feedback from our early users. Our goal: make it easier than ever to launch, manage, and grow your communities online.

From the first line of code, we focused on speed, reliability, and a delightful user experience. Whether you're a solo founder or a growing team, our platform is built to scale with you. 💡

**Highlights:**

- ⚡ Fast, responsive dashboard UI for effortless navigation
- 🔐 Security is taken into concern
- 💳 No paywalls or such
- 🎨 Modern design system powered by Nuxt UI
- 📱 Mobile-friendly and accessible from day one
- 📚 Helpful documentation and in-app tips
- 🗔 Studio for content editing
- 🇫🇮 i18n Localization options

**Behind the scenes:** 🛠️

We've using fresh technology to ensure performance. Continuous deployment makes sure you'll always have the latest features and fixes.

We're just getting started—your feedback will help shape the future of our platform. Let us know what you think and what you'd like to see next! ✨
